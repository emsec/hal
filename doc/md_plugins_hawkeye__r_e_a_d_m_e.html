The Hardware Analyzer - a comprehensive reverse engineering and manipulation framework for gate-level netlists.
HAWKEYE

This plugin was developed in the context of the academic publication titled "HAWKEYE - Recovering Symmetric Cryptography From Hardware Circuits" by Gregor Leaner, Christof Paar, Julian Speith, and Lukas Stennes published at IACR Crypto'24. You can find the paper on IACR ePrint (free of charge) and SpringerLink.

For an example application of HAWKEYE and to reproduce the results from the paper, we refer the reader to the paper artifacts hosted by the IACR.
