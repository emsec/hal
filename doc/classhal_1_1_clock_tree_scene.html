Detailed Description

The scene that holds the drawing of a clock tree: a line for every connection and a dot for every gate.

Gates can be highlighted by a ring around them. The ring has the same size on the screen at every zoom, so a single gate can be found in a drawing of the whole chip, where the dot of a gate is far smaller than a pixel.

The scene paints no background of its own, the view shows its own. Chip coordinates have their origin in the lower left corner, the scene's y axis points down. The scene therefore stores the chip's y coordinate negated, use fromChip and toChip to convert.
